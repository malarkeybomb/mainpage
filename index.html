Back in ink

Malarkey Bomb! started in 2003 as a hand-drawn comic about Jack King, his best friend Ace Queen, and a group of friends with no plan at all.

Now the crew is back as animated shorts, drawn in the same flat-color, black-ink style as the original strips.
